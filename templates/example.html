How to Read a Prediction

A breakdown of the key components of a prediction, and how to interpret them.

Matchup
Away team on top, home team on bottom. Whichever team the model favors to win is highlighted.
Model Spread
Our model's own predicted margin. "Ohio State -9.5" means the model expects Ohio State to win by 9.5 points.
Vegas Line
The actual sportsbook spread for the same game, for comparison. Shows N/A if a line isn't posted yet.
Edge
How far our model's line disagrees with Vegas. A bigger edge means a bigger gap between our number and the market's — and more potential value if the model is right.
Win %
The model's estimated probability that its predicted winner actually wins the game.
